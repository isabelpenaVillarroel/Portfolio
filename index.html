GRAPHIC DESIGN

Influx Flow

01 INFLUX FLOW.

This project visualizes the dynamic atmosphere of Calle Carlos Arniches during a busy Rastro Sunday afternoon. The map layers represent the flow of people, highlighting the busiest areas and the distribution of local shops and street market stands.

→ JAN. 2025

The Namib Custodians

02 THE NAMIB CUSTODIANS.

This project proposes the implementation of the Namib Custodians; a family of robots designed to work alongside humans in protecting the delicate ecosystems of the Namib Desert, particularly from the impact of human activities like filmmaking by creating accountability.

→ DEC. 2024

Machona & Puñalito

03 MACHONA & PUÑALITO.

Gensco is a fanzine dedicated to dismantling rigid gender expectations through bold storytelling and cultural critique. At its core, it follows the journey of Machona and Puñalito, two Luchadores who defy the constraints imposed upon them.

→ MAY. 2023

MATERIAL EXPLORATION

Grounding Cable

04 GROUNDING CABLE.

This project transforms discarded grounding cables through non-chemical and chemical processes to create new materials. The results showcase innovative, sustainable applications that repurpose waste and reduce environmental impact.

→ JAN. 2025

Glass Blowing Mold

05 GLASS BLOWING MOLD.

Inspired by clay’s moldable and adhesive properties, we were interested in designing a mould that experimented with linking diverse forms. We created isolated bubbles in thirty-one cylindrical forms and stuck them together.

→ MAY. 2025

PRODUCT DESIGN

Kiri Table

06 KIRI TABLE.

This project uses Kirigami-inspired techniques to transform flat panels into functional 3D structures. The Kiri Table’s strategic cuts and locking mechanism create a collapsible yet sturdy design that blends adaptability, functionality, and sustainability.

Featured in Dezeen Magazine

→ DEC. 2024

Kofuku Bag

07 KOFUKU BAG.

The Kofuku Bag, inspired by the Japanese concept of happiness, reimagines work by fostering a connection with nature. Designed as a versatile, mobile workspace, it enhances comfort and productivity anywhere. I personally sewed the bag and its functional gadgets to ensure practicality and adaptability.

→ DEC. 2022
